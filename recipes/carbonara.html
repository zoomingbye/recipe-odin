A Classic Italian Pasta Dish

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti - 500g
  • Eggs - 2 egg yolks + 2 egg whites
  • Parmesan cheese - 200g
  • Guanciale or pancetta - 100g

Instructions

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions.
  2. While the pasta is cooking, whisk together the egg yolks, egg whites and Parmesan cheese.
  3. Heat the guanciale or pancetta in a large pan until crispy.
  4. Drain the pasta, reserving some pasta water.
  5. Combine the pasta with the guanciale or pancetta.
  6. Remove from heat and stir in the egg and cheese mixture.
  7. Add pasta water as needed to create a creamy sauce.
