A Simple Macaroni Dish

Ingredients

  • Macaroni - 250g
  • Milk - 500ml
  • Cheddar cheese - 200g, grated
  • Butter - 2 tablespoons
  • Flour - 2 tablespoons
  • Salt and pepper - to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook the macaroni according to the package instructions, then drain it.
  2. Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat.
  3. Stir in the flour and cook for one minute.
  4. Gradually whisk in the milk until the sauce is smooth and thickened.
  5. Stir in the grated cheddar cheese until melted.
  6. Season with salt and pepper, then combine the sauce with the macaroni.
